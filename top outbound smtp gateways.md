## 🛡️ Universal Prerequisite: DNS Authentication Records

To satisfy **Google and Yahoo's mandatory deliverability requirements**, every custom sending domain must configure three records in their DNS provider (Cloudflare, GoDaddy, Namecheap, Route 53):

### 1. SPF (Sender Policy Framework)
A TXT record that authorizes your gateway’s servers to send mail for your domain:
* **Host/Name:** `@` (or subdomain like `mail`)
* **Type:** `TXT`
* **Value (Example for multiple providers):**
  ```text
  v=spf1 include:amazonses.com include:resend.com include:sendgrid.net include:spf.brevo.com include:_spf.google.com ~all
  ```
  *(Only include the services you are actively using).*

### 2. DKIM (DomainKeys Identified Mail)
A cryptographic signature generated by your provider. You will add 1 to 3 `CNAME` or `TXT` records generated in your provider console.

### 3. DMARC (Domain-based Message Authentication)
Tells recipient servers what to do if SPF or DKIM fails:
* **Host/Name:** `_dmarc`
* **Type:** `TXT`
* **Value:**
  ```text
  v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc-reports@yourdomain.com; pct=100
  ```

## 1. 🔶 Amazon SES (Simple Email Service)

Amazon SES is the most cost-effective solution for sending emails at scale ($0.10 for 1,000 emails).

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Setup:
1. **Create AWS Account & Open SES Console:**
   * Go to [AWS Console](https://console.aws.amazon.com/ses) and select your nearest region (e.g., `us-east-1` or `eu-west-1`).
2. **Verify Your Domain:**
   * Navigate to **Configuration** $\rightarrow$ **Identities** $\rightarrow$ **Create Identity**.
   * Select **Domain**, enter your domain name (e.g., `yourcompany.com`).
   * Select **Easy DKIM** (RSA 2048-bit).
   * Copy the 3 generated `CNAME` records and paste them into your DNS manager.
3. **Generate SMTP Credentials:**
   * In the left menu, click **Account Dashboard** or **SMTP Settings**.
   * Click **Create SMTP Credentials**.
   * Enter an IAM user name (e.g., `ses-campaignstack-user`) and click **Create User**.
   * **IMPORTANT:** Download or copy the **SMTP Username** and **SMTP Password** (these are different from your AWS IAM Access Keys).
4. **Request Production Access (Move Out of Sandbox):**
   * By default, your SES account is in "Sandbox Mode" (can only send 200 emails/day to pre-verified emails).
   * Go to **Account Dashboard** $\rightarrow$ click **Request Production Access**.
   * Select **Marketing** or **Transactional**, provide your website URL, and explain your opt-in/unsubscribe process. Approval usually takes 4–24 hours.

### 🔌 CampaignStack Connection Settings:
* **Name:** `Amazon SES - US East`
* **Host:** `email-smtp.us-east-1.amazonaws.com` *(match your chosen AWS region)*
* **Port:** `587`
* **Encryption:** `tls` (or `465` with `ssl`)
* **Username:** `[Your Generated SES SMTP Username]`
* **Password:** `[Your Generated SES SMTP Password]`
* **Sender Email:** `hello@yourcompany.com` *(must match your verified domain)*
* **Sender Name:** `Your Name or Brand`

## 2. ⚡ Resend

Resend is a developer-first email platform with fast DNS verification and high deliverability.

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Setup:
1. **Sign Up:**
   * Create an account at [resend.com](https://resend.com). Free tier gives **3,000 emails/month** (100 emails/day).
2. **Add Domain:**
   * Go to **Domains** $\rightarrow$ click **Add Domain**.
   * Enter your domain (e.g., `yourcompany.com` or `send.yourcompany.com`).
   * Resend provides:
     * 1 `MX` record (for return-path/bounces)
     * 1 `TXT` record (SPF)
     * 1 `TXT` record (DKIM)
   * Add them to your DNS manager. Click **Verify DNS**. Verification is usually instant.
3. **Generate API Key / SMTP Password:**
   * Go to **API Keys** $\rightarrow$ click **Create API Key**.
   * Set Permission to **Full Access** or **Sending Access**.
   * Copy the generated key (e.g., `re_123456789...`).

### 🔌 CampaignStack Connection Settings:
* **Name:** `Resend Primary Gateway`
* **Host:** `smtp.resend.com`
* **Port:** `465` (or `587`)
* **Encryption:** `ssl` (for 465) or `tls` (for 587)
* **Username:** `resend`
* **Password:** `re_your_api_key_here`
* **Sender Email:** `newsletter@yourcompany.com`
* **Sender Name:** `Your Brand`

## 3. 🦅 Twilio SendGrid

SendGrid provides 100 free emails per day forever.

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Setup:
1. **Sign Up:**
   * Register at [sendgrid.com](https://sendgrid.com).
2. **Sender Authentication (Domain Verification):**
   * Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **Sender Authentication**.
   * Under "Domain Authentication", click **Get Started**.
   * Choose your DNS host and select whether you want SendGrid to brand links.
   * Add the 3 provided `CNAME` records to your DNS provider to validate DKIM and Return-Path.
3. **Generate API Key:**
   * Go to **Settings** $\rightarrow$ **API Keys** $\rightarrow$ click **Create API Key**.
   * Choose **Restricted Access** $\rightarrow$ give **Full Access** to **Mail Send**.
   * Copy your API Key (e.g., `SG.xxxxxxxx...`).

### 🔌 CampaignStack Connection Settings:
* **Name:** `SendGrid Marketing SMTP`
* **Host:** `smtp.sendgrid.net`
* **Port:** `587`
* **Encryption:** `tls`
* **Username:** `apikey` *(literal string: `apikey`)*
* **Password:** `SG.your_actual_api_key`
* **Sender Email:** `news@yourcompany.com`
* **Sender Name:** `Your Company`

## 4. 🚀 Brevo (Formerly Sendinblue)

Brevo offers **300 free emails every day** (9,000/month) with no monthly contract and an easy setup.

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Setup:
1. **Sign Up:**
   * Register at [brevo.com](https://www.brevo.com).
2. **Add & Authenticate Your Senders & Domain:**
   * Click your profile menu (top right) $\rightarrow$ **Senders, Domains & Dedicated IPs**.
   * Under **Domains**, click **Add a domain**.
   * Brevo will display:
     * `brevo-code` (TXT)
     * DKIM record (TXT)
     * DMARC record (TXT)
   * Add them to your DNS and click **Check DNS records**.
3. **Retrieve SMTP Credentials:**
   * Click your profile menu $\rightarrow$ **SMTP & API**.
   * Under the **SMTP** tab, your credentials are ready:
     * **Host:** `smtp-relay.brevo.com`
     * **Port:** `587`
     * **Login:** Your Brevo account email
     * **Master Password:** Click **Generate a new SMTP key** and copy it.

### 🔌 CampaignStack Connection Settings:
* **Name:** `Brevo Daily Broadcast`
* **Host:** `smtp-relay.brevo.com`
* **Port:** `587`
* **Encryption:** `tls`
* **Username:** `your-brevo-login-email@example.com`
* **Password:** `[Your Generated SMTP Key]`
* **Sender Email:** `hello@yourdomain.com`
* **Sender Name:** `Your Name`

## 5. 📬 Google Workspace (Gmail SMTP Relay)

Allows up to **2,000 emails per day** per paid user account. Ideal for high-trust 1-on-1 cold outreach sequences.

### ⚙️ Step-by-Step Setup:

> **Notice:** Standard Google account passwords do not work with third-party SMTP. You must generate a Google **App Password**.

1. **Enable 2-Step Verification:**
   * Go to [myaccount.google.com/security](https://myaccount.google.com/security).
   * Ensure **2-Step Verification** is turned **ON**.
2. **Generate an App Password:**
   * In the Security page search bar, type **"App passwords"** (or go to `myaccount.google.com/apppasswords`).
   * App Name: Enter `CampaignStack`.
   * Click **Create**.
   * Google will show a 16-character code (e.g., `abcd efgh ijkl mnop`). Copy this without spaces.
3. **Configure SPF for Google:**
   * Ensure your domain TXT record includes:
     ```text
     v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com ~all
     ```

### 🔌 CampaignStack Connection Settings:
* **Name:** `Google Workspace Cold Outreach`
* **Host:** `smtp.gmail.com`
* **Port:** `587` (or `465`)
* **Encryption:** `tls` (or `ssl` for 465)
* **Username:** `your-email@yourdomain.com`
* **Password:** `abcdefghijklmnop` *(the 16-character App Password)*
* **Sender Email:** `your-email@yourdomain.com`
* **Sender Name:** `Your Name`

## 🎯 How to Add These to CampaignStack

1. Open CampaignStack and go to **Outbound Accounts** (`/outbound_mail_accounts`).
2. Click **Create Outbound Mail Account**.
3. Fill in the fields matching the connection parameters above.
4. Click **Save Account**.
5. When creating or editing a broadcast (**Newsletters** $\rightarrow$ **Edit Broadcast**):
   * Select your preferred gateway under the **Outbound Mail Gateways** selector.
   * You can select multiple gateways for load distribution or domain rotation.

## 🚨 Essential Deliverability Rules (Never Get Banned)

* **Stay Below 0.1% Spam Complaints:** If more than 1 in 1,000 recipients marks your message as spam, gateways will pause your account.
* **Keep Hard Bounces Under 2%:** Always clean and verify contact lists before importing them.
* **Warm Up New Domains Gradually:**
  * Week 1: 50 emails/day
  * Week 2: 200 emails/day
  * Week 3: 500 emails/day
  * Week 4: 1,500+ emails/day
* **Always Provide an Unsubscribe Option:** Place an opt-out link in your template footer to keep recipients from clicking "Report Spam".
* **Separate Use Cases Across Domains:**
  * Transactional & notifications $\rightarrow$ Domain A (e.g. `mail.domain.com` on Resend/SES)
  * Marketing & newsletters $\rightarrow$ Domain B (e.g. `news.domain.com` on Brevo/SendGrid)
  * Direct 1-on-1 outreach $\rightarrow$ Domain C (Google Workspace)
